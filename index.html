Sizing and warehouse QC checklist

Use a well-fitting item as a reference. Measure it flat in centimeters and use the same method shown on the seller’s chart. When warehouse quality-control photos are available, compare them with the exact variant you ordered.

Shoes
Compare foot length with the chart’s recommended foot length. If the chart gives insole length, ask the seller how much room the fit allows. Check that both shoes show the requested size.
Sweatshirts
Compare chest width, body length and sleeve length. Request close-ups of cuffs and the neckline if their condition is unclear.
T-shirts
Compare shoulder width and body length. For embroidery, zoom in on placement and the stitching around the edges.
Jackets
Measure a jacket that fits over your usual layers. Review zipper alignment, lining and pocket closures in any available QC photos.

Estimate delivered cost

Enter amounts in USD. For international shipping, use a quote for your destination and the agent’s billable parcel weight. Include a provisional amount for any taxes or fees that are still unknown.

LoveGoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the product links go?

Each listing opens its product page on CAFANS. The two main buttons open the CAFANS homepage.

Can I buy every listed item through LoveGoBuy?

Open the item’s buying options and check which agents are available for that listing. Confirm that the agent can purchase the exact variant and ship it to your destination.

Why might my international shipping quote change?

Some routes bill by parcel dimensions when volumetric weight exceeds actual weight. Compare quotes using the same packed dimensions and destination, and check which fees each quote includes.
